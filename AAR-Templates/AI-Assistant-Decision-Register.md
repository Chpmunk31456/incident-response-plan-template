project requirements across conversations.

## 3. Decision Status

- Proposed
- Active
- Under Review
- Superseded
- Withdrawn

## 4. Decision Priority

When instructions conflict:

1. Follow applicable system and safety requirements.
2. Follow the user's latest explicit instructions.
3. Review verified project-specific decisions.
4. Review previously documented preferences.
5. Identify unresolved conflicts before proceeding.

Never silently override a newer decision with an older one.

## 5. Employment Research Decisions

Maintain verified requirements for:

- Eligible work locations
- Remote, hybrid, and onsite arrangements
- Employment structure
- Compensation expectations
- Required qualifications
- Excluded job categories
- Previously reviewed opportunities
- Submitted applications
- Rejections and closed positions

Verify current vacancies against employer sources.

2. Check its effective date.
3. Review newer decisions.
4. Verify relevant supporting evidence.
