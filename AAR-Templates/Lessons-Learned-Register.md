# Cybersecurity Lessons Learned Register

## 1. Purpose

This register documents lessons identified during cybersecurity
After-Action Reviews (AARs), incident investigations, security
exercises, and operational disruptions.

The objective is to improve security controls, incident response,
business resilience, and organizational learning.

All reviews should be evidence-based and conducted without blame.

## 4. What Worked Well?

Document successful activities, including:

- Early incident detection
- Effective escalation
- Successful containment
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Accurate communications
- Recovery procedures
- Security monitoring effectiveness

## 5. What Did Not Work?

Identify opportunities for improvement:

- Detection delays
- Escalation failures
- Incomplete documentation
- Ineffective security controls
- Communication breakdowns
- Recovery delays
- Unclear responsibilities
- Insufficient training

Focus on processes, systems, and controls rather than assigning blame.

## 6. STOP / START / CONTINUE

### STOP

Identify ineffective practices that should be discontinued.

### START

Identify new procedures, technologies, or controls that should
be implemented.

### CONTINUE

Identify effective practices that should be maintained.

## 7. Root Cause Relationships

For each significant lesson, reference:

- Root Cause Analysis findings
- Supporting evidence
- Contributing factors
- Control deficiencies
- Associated corrective actions

## 8. Corrective Action Integration

Transfer approved improvements to:

AAR-Templates/Corrective-Action-Register.md

Each corrective action must include:

- Unique action identifier
- Accountable owner
- Priority
- Due date
- Verification criteria
- Closure approval

## 9. Knowledge Sharing

Determine which lessons should be communicated to:

- Security Operations
- IT Infrastructure
- Network Operations
- Risk and Compliance
- Business Continuity
- Executive Leadership
- OT/ICS Operations
- Third-party service providers

Document training, policy updates, or procedural changes.

## 10. Effectiveness Review

Evaluate whether implemented improvements:

1. Addressed the identified weakness.
2. Reduced the likelihood of recurrence.
3. Improved detection or response capabilities.
4. Reduced operational or business impact.
5. Produced measurable improvements.

## 13. Confidentiality

Do not publish sensitive incident evidence, customer information,
credentials, personal information, or internal security weaknesses
in public repositories.

Store confidential incident records in approved access-controlled
systems.
