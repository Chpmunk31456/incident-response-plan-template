## Action Status

- Open

## Closure Requirements

1. Confirm remediation was implemented.
2. Verify the corrective action resolves the finding.
3. Record supporting evidence.
4. Obtain management approval.
5. Record the closure date.

## Management Review

Review overdue actions, recurring incidents,
and unresolved high-risk findings.
