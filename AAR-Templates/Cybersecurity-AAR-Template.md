## 4. What Happened?

Describe the actual sequence of events.

Identify:

- Initial indicators
- Detection methods
- Escalation decisions
- Response activities
- Containment measures
- Recovery actions

**Use a blameless, evidence-based approach.**

## 5. What Was Supposed to Happen?

Document expected procedures and controls.

Compare actual performance against:

- Incident-response procedures
- Security monitoring requirements
- Escalation protocols
- Business continuity procedures
- Recovery objectives
- Relevant organizational policies

Identify deviations and explain their operational significance.
